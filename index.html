🕷️ SPIDER-MAN SAVES 20 CIVILIANS — BUT WHO PAYS FOR THE DAMAGE?

J. Jonah Jameson Editor-in-Chief
10 Aug 2026 · 10:42 AM

NEW YORK — Once again, the masked vigilante known as "Spider-Man" has left a trail of destruction in his wake while playing hero.

Yesterday's incident at the Queensboro Bridge resulted in an estimated $2 million in damages. While sources confirm that 20 civilians were rescued from a collapsing section of the bridge, the question remains: who will pay for the extensive damage caused by this reckless vigilante?

"He destroyed half the bridge!" said local business owner Marcus Chen. "Sure, people were saved, but now we're stuck with the bill and weeks of traffic chaos."

This latest incident adds to the growing controversy surrounding the web-slinger. While some New Yorkers hail him as a hero, others question whether a masked vigilante operating outside the law is what this city needs.

The NYPD has declined to comment on whether they are investigating Spider-Man's activities. Mayor's office released a statement thanking the "anonymous individual" for assistance but emphasized that vigilantism remains illegal.

"We cannot have masked individuals taking the law into their own hands, regardless of their intentions."

The Daily Bugle will continue to investigate this menace and bring you the truth that others won't report.
